## Completion states

- `measured` — A result was measured and may be stored in the definition’s declared result field.
- `not_measured` — No measurement was taken or supplied.
- `skipped` — The assessment was intentionally skipped.
- `contraindicated` — The assessment was not performed because it was contraindicated.
- `pain_limited` — Pain prevented or limited completion; this is a clinical-review state, not a performance value.
- `unable` — The person could not complete the assessment protocol.
- `redundant` — The source marked the assessment redundant or omitted it in favor of another observation.

None of these states implies numeric zero.
